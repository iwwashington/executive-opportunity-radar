Work

Different rooms. Different responsibilities.

I’ve spent much of my career inside organizations responsible for information people rely on. The work has ranged from running an organization to starting something new inside a larger one to governing institutions at greater scale.

2017–2022

Online News Association

Washington spent 11 years at ONA, including six as CEO. In that role, he was responsible for operations, strategy, partnerships, programs, revenue and the board relationship. ONA20 Everywhere drew about 2,000 participants from roughly 40 countries; in 2022, more than 5,200 people engaged in ONA programs beyond annual membership.

6 years as CEO
Current

KFF

Senior vice president and executive director of KFF’s Health Information and Trust Initiative, a new core program focused on health information, misinformation and trust. Public filings document philanthropic support to KFF for planning and implementation of the initiative.

SVP / Executive Director
2018–present

American Journalism Project

Board member since 2018, vice chair beginning in 2021 and chair since 2025. AJP reports more than $270 million raised since launch, investments in 58 nonprofit news organizations and a portfolio serving 105 communities across 38 states and Puerto Rico.
